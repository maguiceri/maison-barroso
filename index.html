01 — The Founder

Cristina Barroso

your person in Marbella

I'm not a property portal. I'm the person who already knows which street gets the evening sun, which school has a place in September, and which villa never made it online.

Maison Barroso was born from a simple idea: buying a home in Marbella should feel like being welcomed by a friend who knows everyone. I present and sell homes as they deserve to be seen, find the right property for those who are buying, and care for every home as if it were my own — from a stocked pantry to an arrival that feels like coming home.
